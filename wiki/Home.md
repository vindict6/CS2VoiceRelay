# CS2VoiceRelay wiki

CS2VoiceRelay plays your CS2 server's voice chat in a browser tab, with a live scoreboard. The
[README](https://github.com/vindict6/CS2VoiceRelay#readme) covers installing it and every config
setting.

- [Embedding the voice player in your site](Embedding): one script tag, a PHP helper, HTTPS, and
  building your own player.
